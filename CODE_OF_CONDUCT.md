# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include harassment, discriminatory language or imagery,
personal attacks, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that
would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, reject, or edit contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behaviour violates this Code of Conduct. Report a
concern privately to the repository owner. Security vulnerabilities follow the separate
process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
